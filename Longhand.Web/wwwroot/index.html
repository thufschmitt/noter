Settings

Longhand sends your handwriting to OpenAI's models through your ChatGPT subscription, the way the Codex CLI does. Sign in to the Codex CLI with ChatGPT, then paste the contents of ~/.codex/auth.json here. The sign-in stays on the server this page came from. This uses ChatGPT's private Codex service: it may stop working without notice. Longhand renews the sign-in when it runs out, after which the Codex CLI's copy stops working (and the other way round), so you may have to sign in there again.

Each conversation keeps its own model. New ones start with the one you chose last.

Symbols

Symbols are your own shortcuts: a mark you draw in the margin that means something to you. Give it a name and say what it means; the model then does that when it sees the symbol in your writing. There are none until you add some. They are saved as you go.
