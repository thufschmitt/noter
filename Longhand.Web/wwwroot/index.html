Settings

Longhand sends your handwriting to Claude with your Anthropic API key. The key stays on the server this page came from.

Symbols

Symbols are your own shortcuts: a mark you draw in the margin that means something to you. Give it a name and say what it means; Claude then does that when it sees the symbol in your writing. There are none until you add some. They are saved as you go.
